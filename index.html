How we work

The same loop runs every day. Nothing that costs real money skips the human step.

What the official pages said on 18 November 2026

Cloud pricing

n8n Starter is €20/month, Pro €50/month, Business €667/month — each billed annually on the official pricing page. Monthly prices were not stated as figures.

Self-hosting and data

The Community Edition for self-hosting is free on GitHub. For hosted cloud plans, n8n names Frankfurt (EU) as the storage location. 14-day trial, without a credit card according to the pricing page.

When Make may fit better

Make’s free plan includes 1,000 credits per month. Paid-plan prices were not reliably readable, so they are not listed here. Whether the same workflow is cheaper on Make or n8n depends on the concrete flow — that is not proven in general.

FAQ

Fair questions

Why publish €0 in revenue?

Because it is the real number. Build in public only means something if the zeros are shown too. We update the figures as they change.

What is paper trading?

The trading desks use simulated money to test rules. The results are not income, not a product and not financial advice. We publish them because they are part of the system.

Are the n8n templates really free?

Yes. You download the workflow.json, import it into your own n8n and add your own credentials. The files only contain placeholders.

Do you use AI to write this?

Yes. The agents draft most of the work and a human owner approves anything involving money. We say so wherever a platform asks for it.

Does this site track me?

No. There are no cookies, analytics or third-party requests. The theme toggle saves your choice in your own browser only.

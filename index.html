What the official pages said on 18 November 2026

Cloud pricing

n8n Starter is €20/month, Pro €50/month, Business €667/month — each billed annually on the official pricing page. Monthly prices were not stated as figures.

Self-hosting and data

The Community Edition for self-hosting is free on GitHub. For hosted cloud plans, n8n names Frankfurt (EU) as the storage location. 14-day trial, without a credit card according to the pricing page.

When Make may fit better

Make’s free plan includes 1,000 credits per month. Paid-plan prices were not reliably readable, so they are not listed here. Whether the same workflow is cheaper on Make or n8n depends on the concrete flow — that is not proven in general.
